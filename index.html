01 / The idea

Technology that puts the farmer first.

AGROBoT starts with a simple question: how can we make selected agricultural tasks more manageable for people who cannot easily move around a field or perform physically demanding work?

🌾

A physically demanding job

Soil preparation, planting, spraying and harvesting can require considerable physical effort and movement across the field.

♿

Access is not equal

Elderly farmers and people with disabilities or limited mobility may find conventional field activities difficult to perform independently.

Our approach: remote operation + monitoring + modular attachments

Support the farmer's supervision and control while progressively developing more agricultural functions.

Accessibility-led design
02 / Four agricultural features

One platform. Four farming functions.

AGROBoT is planned as a modular vehicle that can support different stages of agricultural work. The status labels below distinguish what has been demonstrated from what is planned.

💧Demonstrated

Spraying Mechanism

A tank, pump and nozzle arrangement designed to spray liquid fertilizers or pesticides over crops, helping reduce the need for manual spraying.

Current prototype: liquid spraying mechanism demonstrated.
🌱Future development

Automatic Seed Planting

A proposed seed-planting attachment intended to place seeds at a selected spacing and depth for a more systematic planting process.

Planned module · not yet implemented in the current prototype.
🚜Future development

Soil Preparation

A proposed mini-tiller attachment intended to loosen and prepare the soil before planting.

Planned module · not yet implemented in the current prototype.
🌾Future development

Smart Harvesting

A proposed crop-maturity sensing and harvesting-assistance mechanism to help identify mature crops and support the harvesting process.

Advanced concept · not yet implemented in the current prototype.

05 / Development roadmap

Build. Validate. Improve.

The next phase is focused on making the prototype more reliable and evaluating it in real agricultural conditions before expanding its capabilities.

Phase 01 · Current

Core prototype

Demonstrate remote movement, live camera monitoring, ultrasonic obstacle detection and liquid spraying.

Phase 02 · Next

Field validation

Test stability, battery performance, reliability and usability on suitable field surfaces; collect user feedback.

Phase 03 · Future

Modular expansion

Explore seed planting, soil preparation, smart harvesting and other advanced assistance features.

06 / Intended impact

More access. More independence.

These are the intended benefits of the project and will need to be assessed through field trials and feedback from potential users.
